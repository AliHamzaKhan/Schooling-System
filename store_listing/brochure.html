What Taleem Hub Does

One secure place for everything a school runs on.

No more scattered notebooks, paper slips and endless group chats. Your school creates the accounts and controls who sees what — so every person gets exactly the view they need, on the device already in their pocket.

Attendance & Alerts

Daily and subject-wise attendance, with an instant notification to guardians the moment a child is absent.

Smart Timetables

Always-current class schedules for everyone, with an easy editor for staff to build and adjust them.

Assignments & Results

Share assignments, publish grades, and let students and guardians follow academic progress over time.

AI-Assisted Quizzes

Teachers generate quiz questions in seconds with a built-in AI model — less prep time, more teaching.

Fees & Payroll

Track fee invoices and payment status with automatic reminders, and manage staff salary attendance and payroll.

Books & Notes

Digital books, class notes and study material in one library, with secure sign-in and optional fingerprint or face unlock.
