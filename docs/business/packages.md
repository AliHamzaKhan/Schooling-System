# Meri Taleem — Subscription Packages

One platform to run your whole school — from attendance to fees, exams to
transport. Pick a plan; upgrade any time.

**In every plan:** mobile apps (Android + iOS) for staff, students & guardians,
the Headmaster dashboard, and school-wide messaging.

---

## Basic — *Run your school*
**Starting 299 / school / month · up to 200 students**

- Student, teacher & guardian management — admissions, profiles, ID cards
- Daily & subject-wise attendance
- Timetable & academic calendar
- Announcements + notifications (push, WhatsApp, SMS, email) & parent–teacher chat
- Leave requests & parent–teacher meetings

## Standard — *Add exams & homework*
**Everything in Basic, plus — 799 / school / month · up to 750 students**

- Exam scheduling & datesheets
- Marks entry, grading & report cards
- Homework assignment & submission tracking

## Premium — *Run the whole institution*
**Everything in Standard, plus — 1,499 / school / month · unlimited students**

- Fees — invoices, online collection, receipts & automated reminders
- HR & payroll — salaries, payslips, staff records
- Transport — routes, vehicles, drivers & live GPS tracking
- Inventory & asset tracking
- AI suite — AI exam & quiz generation, performance insights, report remarks
- Advanced reports, analytics & exports
